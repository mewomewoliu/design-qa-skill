Core job partly supportedValue plausible, unvalidated

Snoozing works online, but offline snoozes look confirmed when they are only queued, and supervisors can't see why work moved.

  • P3Spanish snooze label truncates
Full summary

The one-tap path is fast and clear in both themes. Two P1 gaps: offline confirmation overstates what happened, and the supervisor's view loses the reason for a snooze. Decide the reason rule before build.

User value. Technicians report deferring work they can't finish (E01), but no analytics show how often (E03). Validate with a measurement of snooze → completion within 48 h.

P1

F01Offline snoozes look confirmed when they're only queued

Fix before build · Design gap · Observed in demo S02
Should
A clear 'saved on phone, will send when online' state that persists until sync.
Does
Same success toast and list state as online; spec doesn't mention offline.
FixAdd an offline variant of the toast and a pending marker on the task until sync; reuse the app's existing offline banner.
Why it matters & retest

Why it matters. Technician leaves believing the supervisor sees it; supervisor plans around stale data. Frequency unknown, but no-signal rooms are common (E04).

Retest. S02: snooze offline → see pending marker → reconnect → marker clears and supervisor view updates.

P1

F02Supervisors lose the reason and history of snoozes

Fix before build · Design gap · Static evidence S03S06
Should
Supervisors can see why a task moved and whether it keeps moving.
Does
Reason is optional and device-local; each snooze overwrites the last.
Decision neededDecide how much reason capture to require before build. Recommend B if supervisors confirm they plan from reasons.
AOptional free-text reason, shared

Lowest friction; reasons often blank.

BRequired quick reason chips (No parts / No access / Other)

One extra tap; structured, reportable.

CNo reason; show snooze count and history only

Zero friction; supervisor sees pattern but not cause.

Why it matters & retest

Why it matters. Snooze can hide exactly the stale work the feature was meant to surface (intent: problem).

Retest. S03 and S06 with the chosen option.
